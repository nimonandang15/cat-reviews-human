Write one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters. A judge model compares the lines two at a time and the funniest take the paid places. Free to enter, and one line is the whole job.

## The review

> One star. He bought me a $90 bed, then slept in my spot on the warm laptop. I have reported him to the sun.

One line, 107 characters: a deadpan one-star review with a specific, recognisable complaint — the expensive bed ignored, the warm laptop stolen. The turn is the last sentence: the cat has escalated the complaint to the sun, the highest feline authority.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (107 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-line, 1–200-character gate.
